## Direction 1 — Methodology and decision semantics

**Result: PASS.**

The assessment remains a 32-control, eight-domain, bilingual release-readiness and evidence-gap instrument with 16 Anti-AI-Slop diagnostic indicators. It intentionally produces no aggregate security/compliance/maturity score.

`BLOCKED`, `CONDITIONAL` and `READY` remain structural gate states. A `READY` result is not a product-security conclusion. PILOT-001 exercises all three gate outcomes, INPUT-001 hardens the assessment-input boundary, EVIDENCE-001 binds declared evidence bytes to an exact local Git commit, VERIFICATION-001 adds signed review provenance, and PILOT-002 exercises their composition.

Residual boundaries remain explicit: factual truth of evidence and `NOT_APPLICABLE` rationales is not established automatically; no real product is independently assessed by the synthetic pilots.

## Direction 3 — Assurance and provenance boundaries

**Pre-remediation result: CONDITIONAL.**
**Post-remediation target: PASS.**

### A-01 — Cross-stage descriptive subject-label drift

VERIFICATION-001 already required exact evidence-manifest SHA-256 and exact subject commit, but it did not require the signed record's untrusted `repository_hint` label to equal the EVIDENCE-001 report label.

This was not a remote-repository identity bypass: `repository_hint` is explicitly non-authenticating. However, accepting conflicting labels in one signed chain could confuse a human reviewer.

**Remediation:** require exact `repository_hint` equality between the signed verification record and EVIDENCE-001 report. Add fail-closed regression cases to VERIFICATION-001 and PILOT-002.

### A-02 — Hard-coded assessment version in VERIFICATION-001

`verify_verification.py` hard-coded `0.1.1` instead of reading the current assessment contract from `data/meta.json`.

**Remediation:** bind `assessment_id` and `assessment_version` to `data/meta.json`, matching the EVIDENCE-001 approach. This does not change the current assessment contract.

Residual boundaries remain unchanged: `allowed_signers` enrolment does not prove a real-world person or organization, an `EXTERNAL` relationship is a signed claim rather than independently proven organizational independence, hash binding does not establish evidence truth, and neither verifier authorizes release or establishes product security.

## Direction 5 — Release, version and tag contract

**Pre-remediation result: HOLD.**
**After version-semantics remediation: GO FOR VERSION-BUMP STAGE.**

The repository currently has two distinct version concepts:

1. **Repository/toolkit release version** — Git tag and `CITATION.cff` release metadata.
2. **Assessment contract version** — `data/meta.json["version"]`, consumed by assessment inputs, evidence manifests and verification records.

The v0.2.0 candidate adds input hardening, artifact binding, signed provenance and end-to-end integration without changing the 32-control model, state model or gate semantics. Therefore the assessment contract remains `0.1.1` unless those assessment semantics are deliberately revised.

AUDIT-002 marks `data/meta.json["version"]` explicitly with `version_scope: "assessment-contract"` and documents the separation in README/PROVENANCE.
